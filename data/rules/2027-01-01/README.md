# PREVIEW — 2027-01-01 (proposed, not enacted)

This directory is **not** a CRA T4127 edition.

It is a labelled preview of the 1 January 2027 CPP rate cut announced in the
Spring Economic Update 2026 (2026-04-28). The 2027 T4127 and the 2027 YMPE /
YAMPE have not been published. Maximums here reuse the 2026 ceilings
($74,600 / $85,000) so the rate change can be computed without inventing
indexed dollars.

BC personal-income-tax brackets and credits stay at 2026 levels (Budget 2026
indexation pause). NL uses the July 2026 BPA of $15,000. PE uses the July
Option 2 full-year sixth-bracket rate (20%), not the Option 1 prorated 21%.

Do not file from numbers produced under this set.
