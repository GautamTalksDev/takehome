# T4127 January 2026 CSV archive

Byte-for-byte copies of the Chapter 8 CSV files linked from

https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/payroll/t4127-payroll-deductions-formulas/t4127-jan.html

Retrieved `2026-09-12T03:58:28Z`. Hashes in `archive.json` are recorded
**before** ingest. The ingest tool parses only this directory, never a URL.

`supplement.json` holds scalars T4127 publishes in Chapters 2 and 4 that are
**not** in the CSV bundle (BPAF minimum, Ontario factor Y, Ontario V2 health
premium). The parser does not invent them.
